# From a concept to your own product or character

[Back to workflows](../README.md#workflows) · [简体中文](./product-reference_zh.md)

**Use text to explore framing and action; prepare images when product appearance or character identity matters.** Inspect both routes: reference images do not guarantee exact preservation.

## Start with a concept

The homepage text-only exercises use fictional objects, people and settings. Replacing colors, shapes and actions can explore an idea, but does not establish a faithful depiction of a real product.

Make one shot first. Reuse the same appearance description for additional takes and compare them. Reject mismatched bottles, faces or clothing rather than cutting them together just to complete a sequence.

## Work from existing images

The free forms require **both a start and an end frame** for image guidance:

1. Use images you made or have rights to use, and choose the output aspect ratio.
2. Prepare the actual opening and ending compositions. Keep product, identity, wardrobe, background and framing consistent; change only the intended state, such as light position or a hand gesture.
3. If the ending image is missing, photograph or create a matching one. Do not pair unrelated products. Whether identical endpoint images are accepted or useful needs checking in the form and resulting video.
4. Upload both images and describe motion between their actual contents. These slots are endpoints, not separate “product reference” and “background reference” inputs.

**Adapt this template after replacing every bracketed field:**

```text
Create one five-second shot, [16:9 / 9:16 / 1:1].
Begin at the uploaded start frame and finish at the end frame.
Preserve the [specific product or character] and its [fixed shape, color, wardrobe details].
Only [the actual state difference between the images] changes slowly.
The camera [stays fixed or follows one described move].
Hold the last second. Sound: [intended ambience]. Add no objects or lettering.
```

This template has not been generation-tested. Inspect the beginning, middle and end for geometry, labels, color, hands, faces and background. Reject a take that deforms in the middle even if its endpoints look right. Composite approved lettering in editing when exact text matters, and check its fit to the image.

## Build continuity

Prefer trimming opening, action and ending from one usable clip before generating more shots. For a new angle, prepare its matching endpoint pair and compare appearance and lighting across takes.

For identity references, separate product and setting controls, camera-reference video or audio-driven rhythm, read the input requirements in the [advanced prompt library](../prompts/README.md) and choose a compatible workflow. Do not paste a multi-reference brief into a form with only two endpoint slots.

If you have chosen a workflow, trial in its primary tool. ### Commerce and creator ads

Product demonstrations and ad openings for sellers and creators.

<img src="./assets/gallery/midnight-observatory-tea.webp" alt="Commerce and creator ads" width="360">

Source-library mood reference, not a tested workflow output.

**Primary:** [UGC Maker](https://ugcmaker.org/free-minimax-h3/) — Begin with one switch press or product reveal so product and action continuity are easy to inspect.

**Optional support:** [BestImage AI](https://bestimage.ai/free-minimax-h3/) — Explore an alternate product composition; [HeyDream](https://heydream.im/free-minimax-h3/) — Generate one transition shot; assemble it in an editor

**Optional pickups:** [Flyne AI](https://flyne.ai/free-minimax-h3/) — Use when a product reveal is missing; [SeeVido](https://seevido.com/free-minimax-h3/) — Use when a reaction shot is missing; [AITryOn](https://aitryon.art/free-minimax-h3/) — Use for missing fabric-motion shots in apparel ads

**Shortest route:** Write one product action → trial it in UGC Maker and inspect → make the needed shots there → edit; add another tool only for a missing shot.

**Inputs:** Prepare product references and one selling point; for frame guidance supply matching start/end images. Add exact copy and prices in editing.

**Review:** Product appearance, finger contact, sound/action alignment and truthful claims.

**Trial and limits:** Plan 5s / 480p shots. Success rate and cross-tool continuity have not been tested.

## What MiniMax H3 can do

MiniMax H3 combines video and audio generation with text, image and reference-driven control. Its broader workflows cover scene invention, endpoint transitions, character continuity, camera direction and editing. A useful prompt assigns each reference a role and describes events in playback order.

| Need | Model workflow | What to expect from the free directory |
|---|---|---|
| Invent a scene | Text-to-video | Start from one short written action |
| Connect two compositions | First/last-frame generation | Prepare both endpoint images when the free form requires them |
| Transfer identity, movement or sound | Multi-reference generation | Do not assume these free forms accept video or audio references |
| Longer or higher-resolution work | Local H3-Base and hosted workflows | Free-page limits apply separately; API and local compute may cost money |

Official release notes describe 4–15 seconds and a 768-pixel default short edge; 2K uses the H3-Regenerate-2K stage. H3-Context-IR and H3-Regenerate-2K are hosted components. These are **model-workflow facts, not free-plan entitlements**. Read the [official MiniMax repository](https://github.com/MiniMax-AI/MiniMax-H3), [model card](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3), [capability guide](./docs/minimax-h3-overview.md), [deployment guide](./docs/deployment-guide.md) and [API workflow](./docs/api-workflow.md).

## A complete recipe: observatory tea launch

This complete [BRD-001 recipe](./prompts/01-brand-advertising.md#brd-001-midnight-observatory-tea-launch) comes from Flaq AI. It needs a compatible multi-reference, 12–15-second workflow; **do not paste it unchanged into a five-second free form**. It is not presented as a tested success. For a free practice, retain only the starlight reveal over a still bottle.

**Use it for:** A premium launch film that makes a new bottled tea feel precise, calm, and contemporary.

**Mode:** Product image + location image + optional sound reference  
**Format:** 16:9, target 12–15 seconds  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = exact bottle geometry and label; Image 2 = mountaintop observatory architecture and color mood; Audio 1 = tempo and restraint only.

```text
Create a refined launch film for the fictional tea brand “NORTH WINDOW”. The
story takes place before dawn inside a quiet mountaintop observatory. Preserve
the bottle from Image 1 exactly: tall smoked-glass silhouette, pale stone
label, dark green cap, no invented claims. Use Image 2 only for the circular
room, telescope structure, and blue-hour palette.

Beat 1: a narrow band of starlight travels across the observatory table and
reveals the bottle silhouette; camera makes a slow, level push-in. Beat 2:
condensation gathers naturally, one droplet follows the bottle curve, while
the telescope roof opens and cool sky light enters. Beat 3: the bottle remains
still as the horizon changes from indigo to a restrained amber line; settle on
a clean hero frame with safe negative space above.

Lighting is physically motivated, with glass refraction, soft metal
reflections, and realistic condensation. Sound intent: quiet roof mechanism,
distant wind, one low glass tone, no voiceover. Preserve logo spelling and
label placement. Avoid floating ingredients, liquid explosions, label
mutation, aggressive lens flares, extra text, other brands, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Bottle shape, label fidelity, reflection continuity, condensation path, and usable copy space.

## Common questions

**Is this a list of genuinely free tools?** The listed pages advertise a free browser route. We checked page statements, not successful generation or backend identity. Queue availability and access can change; report changes with the exact URL and date.

**Does no signup mean no verification?** No. A page may still require human verification before submitting. That does not itself mean an account is required.

**Can I use one photo?** These free-page instructions describe text-only or a pair of start/end images. Do not assume a one-image route just because the broader H3 model supports one.

**Are 2K, 15-second clips or APIs free?** Not established by this directory. VideoWeb’s free page states 5 seconds at 480p. Read each route’s current settings and costs.

**Can I use outputs commercially?** This check does not establish blanket permission. Consult the selected service’s current terms and obtain rights for any people, voices, music, products and inputs. Repository MIT licensing applies to repository material, not every generated output or external video.

**Why did my result drift?** Simplify to one subject action and one camera movement, keep references consistent, and change one instruction at a time. Check hands, geometry, product marks, scene continuity and audio before keeping a take.

**What if a page or video fails?** Do not repeatedly submit while queued. Save your prompt, check the original post for an expired video URL, and [report a broken tool or media link](https://github.com/aivideoweb/best-free-minimax-h3/issues/new?template=tool-update.yml).
